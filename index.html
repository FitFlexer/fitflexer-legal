Consistency, made competitive.

FitFlexer is a fitness app for iPhone. Most fitness apps track what you lifted; FitFlexer tracks whether you turned up — and puts that on a leaderboard. Log your sessions, build a streak, and settle it with your friends.

iPhone · coming to the App Store · contact us

What you can do

Log every session

Build routines, track exercises, sets and reps, and keep your whole training history in one place.

Build a streak

Credit for showing up, not just for personal bests. Streaks reward the habit that actually moves the needle.

Compete in leagues

Ranked leagues with a skill rating that moves as you train. Climb against friends and the wider community.

Run challenges

Set a target, invite people, and settle it. Group challenges with their own chat and leaderboard.

Find your people

Join communities, follow friends, and share progress with people training toward the same thing.

See the change

Weight, body composition and progress photos over time, so the work is visible even on slow weeks.

How FitFlexer uses your Google account

What we receive, and why

You can create a FitFlexer account with Google, or with an email address and password. If you choose Google, we receive only your Google account’s unique identifier, email address, name and profile picture, and we use them for exactly one purpose: to create your FitFlexer account, sign you in, and show your name and picture to the people you train with.

We never read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts or Photos — FitFlexer requests no access to any other Google service. We never use this information for advertising, and we never sell it. The Privacy Policy sets out exactly what we collect, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.
